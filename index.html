mg_02

tape speed, 8:1 · min ¼ · about 9 o’clock ½ · noon normal · max 2× (fig.04)

your input to the outputs

all four heads to the outputs, after their levels

feedback for every head whose button is green

03

tape mechanics

mg_03
[ low cut ]
min = full lows · max = thin, peaky “magnetic drum” repeats
[ tape age ]
min = fresh tape · cw = warmer · max = a peak that blooms at high repeats
[ crinkle ]
friction, creases, splices, dirt · min clean · max chewed
[ wow & flutter ]
ccw = a tuned machine · cw = one that needs service
[ spring ]
spring reverb level · dry and wet both feed it
crinkle and wow & flutter bite much harder at slow speeds

08

self-oscillation

mg_08 · no input needed
1
[ speed / pitch ] fully ccw · [ repeats ] at 100%
2
[ tap ] ×2, as fast as you can · it starts to sing
3
play [ speed ] and the four tape knobs · it wanders through pitches
4

12

dip switches

mg_12 · on the back, off by default
s1 [ feedback cv ]
on: with the transport LED off, the transport jacks toggle feedback buttons 1–4 instead
s2 [ dual split ]
on: left in / out = mono four-head delay · right in / out = mono spring reverb
06

transport

mg_06 · [ transport ] first
[ ∞ ] infinite
record head off · the last delay cycle or loop plays forever
[ ◂▸ ] reverse
from that moment · echo: the whole tape backwards · loop / sample: the loop backwards
[ ▸| ] restart
loop / sample from the top · realigns shift heads · leaves pause instantly
[ ‖ ] pause
the tape slows to a stop, or spins back up · while paused, level 4 = how fast
scrub
while paused, [ speed / pitch ] scrubs about a foot of tape · slower speed = more audio
defaults
all latch except ▸|, always a trigger · change one at power on (mg_15)
